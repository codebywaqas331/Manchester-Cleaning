Should Cleaning Gutters Be A Priority In Altrincham?

Your gutters play an important role around your house. While only a small piece that runs around the edge of your roof, they do a lot more than just "catch" water and keep it from spilling onto you, your friends, and your family. Gutters actually help water drain away from the house and foundation. This helps keep rainwater and any runoff away from your foundation and keeps it from causing damage that can be expensive and time consuming to repair.

But when gutters become clogged and are no longer allowed to drain away from the house, problems begin to arrive. Water may pool and spill over the sides of clogged sections of gutter, sending water down into your foundation and even soaking the siding that surrounds your house. Not only can damage caused to your foundation and siding result in expensive repairs, but clogged gutters can also cause damage to the fascia that sits behind it. Repairing lengths of fascia can cost upwards of £25 a foot, not cheap.

Clogged gutters also cause damage to any flower beds or landscaping you have surrounding your home. Run off can carry away topsoil and erode deep cracks and holes in your garden or flower bed. Lastly, even your gutters themselves can break, which can result in a lot of damage to your home. If this isn't repaired quickly, you'll have water soaking your foundation and siding, and destroying those beautiful flowers you just planted!

A leaking gutter on a home during a rain storm.

As you can see, hiring a gutter cleaner in Altrincham is an important step of the process that should be completed every Spring and Autumn. It's not only going to help you keep water away from those freshly planted flowers, but it will also help keep your foundation and peace of mind intact!

What Abot Gitter Guards?

Gutter guards are simply a grate or cover that keeps large pieces of debris out and lets water in. While some companies may advertise these devices as a fix-all for your home, your gutters will still need to be cleaned once every two years. For a fairly expensive investment, you may be wondering if it's worth it, but once compared to cleaning gutters twice a year, it will end up saving you money, and time if you prefer to clean your own.

While gutter guards do block large debris from entering, dirt, facias, seed pods, and other small materials will still find their way into your gutters. All that debris will need to be cleaned, and your gutter guards will have to be removed in order to do that. Therefore, we recommend gutter guards that are easy to remove and reattach.
