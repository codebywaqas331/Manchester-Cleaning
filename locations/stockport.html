OUR GUTTER CLEANING PROCESS

Our Gutter Cleaning Process

Gutter Cleaning is our specialty and we pride ourselves on our system of cleaning. Below is a step by step outline of what you can expect when we come to your home for your gutter cleaning.

01

Remove Debris from Roof

We remove leaves, acorns, sticks and any other debris from your roof. Many times debris gets caught in the valleys of a roof or behind chimneys. For these situations we utilize extension poles or powerful air blowers to remove it.

02

Hand Remove Debris from Gutters

We remove the heavy debris from the gutters, by hand, and place into a bucket.

03

Flush Gutters

We remove any remaining debris from the gutters with high powered handheld blowers and/or by flushing the gutters with a garden hose. Simply removing the debris by hand, as many companies do, does not adequately remove the smaller particles that are difficult to pick up. At all times, we are careful not to stain the exterior of the gutter. If we do, we promptly clean it up!

04

Test Downspout Drainage

We test each downspout to ensure that it is clear. Note: If a downspout is blocked, we will snake it clear or flush it with a garden hose. Our crews have the experience to disassemble the elbow and unclog the blockage if necessary.

05

Clean Up & Dispose of Debris

We clean up any debris surrounding your property and dispose of it properly. We leave your gutters and the surrounding area “spotless!!”

Stockport, Bramhall, & The Heatons

We specialise in gutter cleaning in Stockport, The Heatons, Bramhall, and Marple. We are able to handle local properties built around steep, rolling hillsides and multi-level urban layouts, particularly around the historic commercial core. We are well equipped to tackle the challenges of clearing gutters that are installed on complex roof designs with staggered levels, narrow internal valleys, and restricted access paths, which can make ladders and scaffolding more complex to use.

Guttering on multi tiered roof designs can be challenging and especially tricky on properties that have higher roof tracks dropping water directly into lower gutter systems. The large leaves and seed keys from the overhanging Sycamore trees drop into these tight connections. The debris eventually gets compressed under the pressure of the falling water to create tight, compacted blockages within internal downpipe joints, leading to a variety of issues such as internal damp.

We worked on gutter blockage issues on a commercial premises that was in the historic Underbanks retail area (SK1). Our team cleared around 45 metres of stepped roof gutter tracking that was completely blocked with compressed organic waste, which was allowing water to pool along an upper brick parapet. We deployed a combination of wireless cameras and high reaching vacuum equipment to resolve the blockages.

We had to tackle clearing of the older guttering system carefully. This meant carefully handling the clean to avoid damaging the lead flashing or the internal joints on the historical lead-lined internal box gutters that were feeding directly into deep, concealed downpipe drops built into the structural brickwork.
We removed the tightly packed organic leaf mould and flushed the entire system.
