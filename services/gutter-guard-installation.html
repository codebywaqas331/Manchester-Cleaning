While gutter guards aren't complex, they can take a while to install. On average, you should expect to be paying around £5-£10 per foot of material, and then an additional £5 to £8 per linear foot installed. The cost of gutter guard installation in Manchester generally matches what you'd expect to pay to install gutters, which means you shouldn't be surprised by a £2,000 quote for a large home.

This depends on the height of your home or business, the amount of rain gutter guards we're going to be working with, and any delays caused by weather or other factors, but you generally shouldn't expect this process to take much longer than 6 hours.

There are several types of screen, filters, and guards that we commonly work with in Manchester, but here's a few of the main categories:

Screens: These are designed to snap into place, are shaped like a shallow "U" and work by blocking large debris from falling into your gutters. These are often made from plastic, aluminium, or PVC-coated metal. While they are one of the more affordable options, they don't generally stand up well to the elements and still allow small debris to pass through the filter.

Solid Covers: These are designed to be either screwed into the fascia or snapped onto the edges of the rain gutters. These gutter guards have a small slit located along the outer edge that allowed water to flow through. While these are another affordable option, they do tend to clog easily and still require extensive cleaning.

Sponges: These are by far the most affordable gutter guards you'll be able to find in Manchester. They are placed inside the gutter and soak in water that is sent to the downspouts without allowing leaves or other debris to follow. While affordable, sponges do become easily clogged with shingle grit and other small debris, and must be replaced instead of cleaned.

Bottle Brush: These gutter guards are designed to keep large debris out of your rain water system and allow air to circulate, preventing rust and holes. These have the appearance of several small "wire brushes" that can be placed inside. While they do a great job keeping out the leaves, they will need to be cleaned at least once a year to prevent clogs and backed up water in your gutters.

Mesh: These products are safe investments for your home, as they do a great job and last much longer than other models. They keep small and large debris at bay by filtering water through hundreds of small holes. These also need to be cleaned at least once a year, but do a great job of protecting your gutters and preventing expensive repairs.
