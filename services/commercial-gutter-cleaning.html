What Are The Symptoms We Should Look For?

If clogged gutters and blocked drainpipes are ignored the problem will not go away. Rain water will not be able to flow down the drainpipes as expected, but may instead pool on roofs, flow down walls and gather at the foundations of the house.

This can lead to roof rot and water leaks to the inside of the property, structural damage, rusting of metal, mildew and mould buildup, as well as insect infestation as mosquitos in particular are attracted to pooling water.

Finding the symptoms of a clogged and blocked gutter and drainpipe before the worst of the above happens can be a bit more difficult for commercial property owners than residential owners, due to the larger extent of guttering that a commercial property has, as well as the positioning (and height) of the gutters themselves.

But some of the clues are as follows, and are very similar to the clues residential owners need to be aware of:

01

Grass Growing From Gutters

Is grass growing from your gutters? Grass needs something to grow in, like soil and water, and a clogged up gutter will have both.

02

Sagging Gutters

Are your gutters noticeably sagging? This could be because of the weight of water drenched dirt, soil, debris and vegetation matter that the gutter was never made to support.

03

Mildew, Rust & Mould

Do you see any mildew, rust and mould around where the gutters are located? These can be visible signs of ongoing moisture and drainage problems.

04

Birds, Rodents & Insects

Do birds, rodents, spiders and insects seem to be particularly attracted to your gutters? A clogged up gutter might provide refuge for rodents from predators. It might also provide nesting material for birds, and insects are often attracted to standing water.
