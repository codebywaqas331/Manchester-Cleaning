Residential Gutter Cleaning Manchester

Gutters are often things we take very little interest in and only think of when they fail horribly at their job. However, you can save a lot of money in gutter repairs by having your gutters and drain pipes regularly cleaned and unclogged before they stop working.

They are pretty simple things: rain falls on the roof, it flows down into the gutters, which leads it to the drain pipe, then off it goes underground and away from the house.

But when gutters and downpipes get clogged up and blocked with dirt, leaves and many other things that join together to become a dam in your drainage system, a normal afternoon downpour can do some real damage.

Blocked Gutters

Water Overflowing From Your Gutters

If you are seeing water overflowing the sides of your gutters rather than flowing down your drainpipe, consider that a sign that there may be a blockage in the system. Water is not meant to just hang around in puddles on your roof, as roofs can rot and metal can rust and cracks can form in your walls, which compounds the problem by bringing the water inside.

Warning Signs

Here are four things to look out for:

So, how can we determine our gutter needs a good clean before things get so bad that we need a roof replacement. Here are four things to look out for:

Are your gutters sagging?

Are your gutters sagging? They are not made to carry a lot of standing weight. Dirt, leaves and vegetation become heavy when laden with water, thus causing them to sag, and giving up a clue that there is a problem.

Have you noticed any mildew, mould or rust up near your roofline?

Have you noticed any mildew, mould or rust up near your roofline? What about around your foundation? This could be an important clue that your water is pooling around or not flowing down your gutters correctly.

Can you see anything growing out of your gutters?

Can you see anything growing out of your gutters? Like grass, or flowers, or plants? Anyone with even the most minor knowledge of how plants work know they need soil to grow. So, if you have greenery growing from your gutters, something is up there allowing that growth to occur. This is a good clue that you need to have your gutters cleaned.

How popular is your gutter to the local rodent or bird population?

How popular is your gutter to the local rodent or bird population? To a scared little rodent wanting to hide away from predators, that tight concealed space filled with warm vegetation is a perfect shelter; to a nesting bird trying to renovate and grow their home, the twigs, leaves and vegetation in your gutter makes it as inviting as Ikea.

Cleaning Gutters Can Be Risky

Cleaning gutters can be risky. If they are blocked and are carrying a heavy load, they may not be stable, so leaning a ladder against a gutter that you don’t know is stable can cause you injury. People can get seriously injured even from a fall of only two metres, and 20 people a year die in Australia from falling off ladders.
